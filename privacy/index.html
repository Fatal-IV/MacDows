MacDows Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Fatal IV

In short: MacDows works on your PC. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send what it reads to the developer. The only thing it can send over the internet is the city you choose for the weather widget, which is off until you choose one.

What MacDows reads on your PC

InformationWhyWhere it goes
Windows notifications (the app that sent it, the title and the text)To show them in the island and in its notification historyStays on your PC. The history is kept in %APPDATA%\MacDows, encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI): it can only be read again by the same Windows user on the same PC, and MacDows keeps no key of its own. You can clear it in the app.
Now playing media (title, artist, cover, progress)To show the player in the islandStays on your PC. A short plain-text log of what played is kept in %APPDATA%\MacDows\logs.
Open windows and programs (titles, icons)To fill the dock and the previewsStays on your PC.
System state (volume, brightness, Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth devices, battery, VPN connections)To show and change them in the Control CenterStays on your PC.
A Wi-Fi password you type to join a networkTo join the networkHeld in memory only and handed to Windows. MacDows does not store it.

What MacDows sends over the internet

  • Weather widget (optional, off until you choose a city). When you search for a city, the name you type is sent to Open-Meteo (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com). To show the weather, the latitude and longitude of the city you chose are sent to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com). MacDows does not use your device location. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to these requests.
  • Updates. The Microsoft Store build is updated by the Microsoft Store. The version downloaded from GitHub checks github.com/Fatal-IV/MacDows for a new release; GitHub receives the usual information of a web request, such as your IP address.

MacDows does not sell or share your information, and it does not send your notifications, media, windows or settings anywhere.

Your choices

  • Clear the notification history inside the app, or delete the folder %APPDATA%\MacDows.
  • Turn the weather off: drag the weather widget out of the island into the widget tray in the island's widget settings. Nothing is sent to Open-Meteo after that.
  • Deny or withdraw MacDows' access to notifications in Windows Settings → System → Notifications.
  • Uninstalling MacDows removes the app; delete %APPDATA%\MacDows to remove what it saved.

Children

MacDows does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published on this page. Questions: contact the publisher through github.com/Fatal-IV.

MacDows internete ne gönderir

  • Hava durumu bileşeni (isteğe bağlı, şehir seçene kadar kapalı). Bir şehir aradığınızda yazdığınız ad Open-Meteo'ya (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com) gönderilir. Hava durumunu göstermek için seçtiğiniz şehrin enlem ve boylamı Open-Meteo'ya (api.open-meteo.com) gönderilir. MacDows cihazınızın konumunu kullanmaz. Bu istekler için Open-Meteo'nun kendi gizlilik politikası geçerlidir.
  • Güncellemeler. Microsoft Store sürümünü Microsoft Store günceller. GitHub'dan indirilen sürüm, yeni sürüm için github.com/Fatal-IV/MacDows adresine bakar; GitHub, bir web isteğinin olağan bilgilerini (örneğin IP adresinizi) alır.

MacDows bilgilerinizi satmaz veya paylaşmaz; bildirimlerinizi, medyanızı, pencerelerinizi veya ayarlarınızı hiçbir yere göndermez.

Seçenekleriniz

  • Bildirim geçmişini uygulamadan temizleyin veya %APPDATA%\MacDows klasörünü silin.
  • Hava durumunu kapatın: adanın widget ayarlarında hava durumu widget'ını adadan widget tepsisine sürükleyin. Bundan sonra Open-Meteo'ya hiçbir şey gönderilmez.
  • MacDows'un bildirimlere erişimini Windows Ayarlar → Sistem → Bildirimler'den reddedin veya geri alın.
  • MacDows'u kaldırmak uygulamayı siler; kaydettiklerini silmek için %APPDATA%\MacDows klasörünü silin.
